All five project pages use a concise overview, resource links, a primary architecture diagram, and optional native disclosure sections for deeper reading. Engineering decisions come first under Go deeper, with short bullets describing choices and tradeoffs. Projects are ordered newest first by year and month. NetAnomaly was removed from the portfolio at the owner's request; its repository is unchanged. This extends the owner's approved 2026-10-09 direction after reviewing their ChatterBox Architecture PDF.

The diagrams borrow the reference's visual organization: grouped boundaries, labeled links, restrained colors, and monospaced technical annotations. They are theme-aware inline React/SVG figures rather than PDF embeds or separate SVG assets. Smaller screens can pan within each diagram without widening the page.

ChatterBox's topology represents the repository's documented single-instance deployment model, not a verified live deployment. Its optional uploads and out-of-band notification services are shown separately. The collapsed sections describe the persistence/broadcast order, core relational model, Redis responsibilities, notification trade-offs, and single-process constraints. The other diagrams summarize the source-backed architecture rather than claim production deployment.
